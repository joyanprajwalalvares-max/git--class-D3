Once there was a curious boy who loved building small machines. 🤖
One day he found an old broken robot in his garage.
Instead of throwing it away, he decided to repair it.
After many hours of work, the robot suddenly turned on.
The robot thanked the boy for fixing it.
They became best friends and started inventing new gadgets together.
Soon their inventions helped people in their town.
Everyone admired the boy’s creativity and kindness.
The robot learned about friendship and teamwork.
And together, they proved that curiosity can change the world. 🌟
